Systems:

- benedict1399: Simple substitution, one invented sign per letter (c and i share one sign, u/v one), no word division, cipher runs inside clear Latin; one name sign (dotted V = King of Aragon) and groups of null separator signs. Puig broke it in 1920; key rebuilt here from the plate.
- siena1421_no14: Italian; substitution with a few homophones (e, o, i, a), a point after every cipher word, two or three code signs for persons (7H, Z7, CIRC) and numbers in cipher digits; key recovered ciphertext-only here (homsolve2).
- florence1429: Homophonic substitution of graphic signs (two or three per letter) with about 17 nulls and a nomenclator of about 30 signs (persons, places, 'che', 'per', 'con'); about twenty short runs inside clear Italian. Key: Gabbrielli 1863 (Meister 1902) extended here from sibling glosses.
- r1854_p1: Unknown. Three cipher runs of letter-like invented signs inside clear Latin, with separator marks (// : ... . |) between groups; none of the Gonzaga register's Malatesta keys fits; not monoalphabetic or homophonic in Latin/Italian under every test run.
- r1854_p2: Latin; homophonic substitution of graphic signs and numerals, 13 null signs, syllable signs (3 = con, 8t = rum), a word sign (S = et), two code signs (ARW = cardinalis, ARO), occasional dot-group separators; whole letter in cipher; key recovered ciphertext-only here.
- r1854_p3: Italian; homophonic substitution of graphic signs with about ten null signs and three name codes (MALx = signor Malatesta, FIOL = fioli del signor marchese, PAPA); runs inside clear Italian; contemporary interlinear decipherment.
- r1854_p4p5: Latin; homophonic substitution of graphic signs (up to four signs per letter), three signs for the word et (E, C, J), syllable signs m = rum and 3c = com; no word division, no nulls found; key recovered ciphertext-only here.
- maria1435: Unknown. Three short cipher runs of digit-like, letter-like and figure signs inside a Catalan letter; dotted figures (sq, bp, O) may be nomenclator signs (hypothesis from the 1429 key).
- it1583_f68: Graphic-sign cipher of the Sforza family (homophonic letters with syllable and word signs, cf. ASMi Sforzesco cart. 1597 no. 14), dot separators, stretches inside clear Lombard Italian.
- it1583_f70: Same sign family as f. 68, a different hand; almost all cipher.
- siena1421_no07: Mixed digits, letters and drawn signs, homophonic-looking (faint glosses give o for several signs), runs inside a clear Italian despatch; key R4750 (Milan 1454) does not fit.
- siena1421_no04: Substitution of capitals, digits and drawn signs in 15 short runs inside a clear Italian letter, plus Latin code words (Calor = duca, storps = Lucca, cras = parte, vapor = cardinali ...); key rebuilt from an alphabet fragment below the text and the recipient's glosses.
- siena1421_no11: Florentine cipher of graphic signs (44 types), no word division, two cipher blocks inside a clear letter; homophonic anneal fails; no key known.
- buda1489: Italian; homophonic substitution of cursive letters, numerals and marks with syllable signs (letter + superscript: q^e = re, b^o = no, m^o = si ...), nulls (w, ÷, -, lone dots) and a small nomenclator (M. = Ser.mo Re, S. = S.V.); no word division (Somogyi 2016 key, extended here).
- siena1421_no06_24: About 65 sign types (letters, digits, drawn signs, signs with a cross above), no word division, frequent 'p p' pairs and point-framed single signs; not plain homophonic substitution (validated solvers fail): probably syllabic or nomenclator elements. No key.
- siena1421_no20_23: About 50-67 sign types, mostly letter-shaped, with superscripts (o^n, q^o ...); no word division; 'f' + o^n / a / o groups behave as units (syllabic or nomenclator element); not plain homophonic substitution. No key.
- siena1421_no25: Italian (Sienese spelling); simple monoalphabetic substitution, one sign per letter, 21 signs built on a running horizontal stroke, a point after each cipher word, one code sign (TH); key recovered ciphertext-only here.
