A z value is the score of the named key against the whole 57,600-key family, measured after the corrections listed.
Recurring clerk confusions: **g for q** (by far the most common), s/x, k/h, m/n, l/t (in `lemi` for 軍, twice), p/b, z/b, o/a.
"Correction" means a change that the context supports and that is also a plausible misreading of the handwriting. [?] marks a doubtful spot.
Dates at the end of a message are rhyme-day (韻目) codes: 有 = 25, 支 = 4.

## fr 28 (a): Tokyo → Junzaburo Shanghai, 25 Mar 1916
Forwarded. No. 07035, 22 words, 25/3/1916 9.35 pm, sent 2 am. Signed 中山.
Transcription (20 words):
mekudituvi repigokofo po[p]adidoti binoputemi himesicazi bexigotahu bukubofome kezizatibi bugehozane kubugafope bigidizizi merijusifi si[q]ezazeqo ju[n]acetibi cicibuzibu tikavomeno xidofanivi jotixayojo mojegojoba qivopotopu

Key: **shanghai1916**. z = 8.9 after corrections (7.6 as first read).

Corrections:
- The first word is written "mekuditwi": the "w" is "uv", giving 電悉. The k also looks like x.
- `poga`→`popa` gives 款. The same word, 款 = popa, recurs in fr 34.
- The final g of siqezazeqo is q (機), and so is the g of siqe (可).
- `juma`→`juna` (m/n) gives 相機而動.
- `bagi`→`baqi` gives 要. The image has "qivopotopu".

Decode:
電悉。前因滬款急，動沉[?]軍已另供允[?]策。今痼[?]雖但[?]動，亦無阿予[?]。王懂[?]必來商。厂[卽?]可相機而動。惟一三艦隊兄處切勿接洽爲要。文。有(25)

Uncertain spots: nopu 沉, bofo 痼, ziza 但, neku/buga 阿予, bigi 懂, sifi 厂. The LM prefers 清 for bofo and 各 for sifi, but the image does not support either. The sense is clear: Shanghai funds are urgent; act as opportunity allows; do not deal with the 1st and 3rd fleets at your end.
