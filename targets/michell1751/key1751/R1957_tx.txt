# NOTE hands/styles: TWO styles. (A) p1 heading, opening and the cipher of p1 ll.1-5 plus the start of l.6 ("3292, 70"): a slanted cursive hand, groups separated by COMMAS, lines sloping down to the right. (B) from "838." in p1 l.6 to the end of p2: an upright hand, groups separated by full stops (occasional commas/colons, e.g. "111:" p1 l.11, "190 -" p1 l.18). The switch is mid-line in p1 l.6 after "3292, 70 ." (wider gap, darker/upright figures from 838 on). Both use the same theta-shaped 8, so possibly one scribe changing pen/style; not decided.
# NOTE hand A digits: 1 mostly with a crossbar or head flag (looks like 't'/'+': 16, 140, 169, 4310, 102, 3184, 104, 1003, 138, 2125, 184, 1550); 8 = narrow oval with an inner vertical/diagonal stroke (theta form: 858, 2382, 1822, 3184, 86, 538, 894, 5814, 258, 138, 5780, 184) - 0 is a small round 'o' (6203, 2070, 140, 4310); 7 = ')' with a small top hook, running below the baseline (5740, 70, 4074, 5760, 2070, 2207, 5701, 1570), in 47 a '7' with a blob-started top bar; 4 open 'A'/'a'-like; 5 's' with flag; 9 with long tail that loops back UNDER the group (409, 894, 169, 3292) - these loops are 9-tails, not circles round the groups; 6 tall ascender - in '60' (l.2) and '6063' (l.5) the ascender of the 6 loops over and runs down into the following 0 (looks like a 'd'/'∂' ligature), read 60/6063 in both places (6063 also frequent in 098R). No circled groups found in the cursive lines.
# NOTE hand B digits: 8 written both as figure-8 and as the theta form (5814, 284, 587, 184, 1804, 5878, 1298, 6058, 1989, 86, 82, 890, 838, 1468, 5884) - every 8/0 decision checked at 4-6x; 0 small, often raised; 1 plain stroke, often i-dotted (111, 1941, 6129, 1763, 2130), sometimes with a hairline lead-in from lower left (15 and 2139 in p1 l.15); 4 = 'u'/'ɥ'-form or closed 'A'-form (4283, 184 p2 l.9); 7 = top bar starting with a blob + long descender below the baseline (decides 7 vs 2: 712, 6472, 3197); 2 sometimes written '?'-like with a dot below the foot (3125 p1 l.6, 1240 p1 l.11, 3426 p1 l.12 - read 2: flat foot present, no descender); 5 's' with a separate flag tick; 9 closed bowl + long tail.
