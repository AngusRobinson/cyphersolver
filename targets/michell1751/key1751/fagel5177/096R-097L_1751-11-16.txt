# 095R right-edge column (ends of lines 1-26 beside the slip) checked against 096R: identical groups and marks (incl. 84=, the blotted 4?8, the bar over 62).
# NOTATION: _ single underline, = double, # triple; ! bar/overline; [del:...] struck; ? uncertain digit; {...} = editorial note on the preceding group (not part of it); "..." clear text.
# NOTE hand: 0 = small plain oval; 8 = oval with a tall entry stroke/upper loop (looks like theta/delta); 6 = oval with tall ascender; 4 = open 'a/u' shape; 5 = 's' shape with flag. 0/8 is the main risk; every 0/8 was checked at 2.5-5x.
# NOTE dots: the scribe often dots the digit 1 like an i (e.g. 591' 2179 811 131 414 418 1011 111 3186 461' 6010 1510). These i-dots are NOT encoded as '^' (they occur only on 1s and look like a letter habit). No other dot/comma-above marks seen.
