# Fagel 5177 (NL-HaNA 1.10.29 inv. 5177), scan 101R (complete letter on one page; lower part blank). 101L = blank verso of 100R (mirror bleed-through only).
# Heading: "Lettre du Roi de Prusse au Secretaire Michell."
# Clear opening: "J'ai bien reçu votre raport du 9 de ce mois" then cipher ("raport" with one p as written; cedilla visible in "reçu")
# Closing line: "... 4183. à Potsdam ce 23 Novemb: 1751"
# Signature: "(Signé.) Federic." (bracket form '(Signé.:)' with a colon-like mark)
# Letter date: Potsdam 23 Nov 1751 (reply to Michell's report of 9 Nov).
# NOTATION: _ single underline, = double, # triple; ! bar/overline; [del:...] struck; ? uncertain digit; {...} = editorial note on the preceding group (not part of it); "..." clear text.
# NOTE hand: same copyist as 098R (upright, full-stop separators, 7 with hooked top, angular open 4, plain-oval 0, two-loop 8, 5 with a separate flag tick). Not the hand of 099R/100R. 0/8 is unproblematic in this hand; the risks are 4/7 and 3/5 (checked at 4-9x).
# NOTE separators: dots occasionally missing (l.2 '7 334 444', '975 258'; l.8 '2255 105'; l.10 '86 2326'); l.4 '169:' has a colon; l.12 '247-' a dash; l.6 and l.14 end with a low dash after the last group (period), not an underline.
# NOTE l.1: 2138 - the 1 is joined to the 3 at the foot; after 2138 a small arrow-like flourish '>' at the line end (continuation sign, not encoded).
# NOTE l.2: '7 334' read as two groups (gap, no dot); 334 has FOUR strokes under it (encoded '#').
# NOTE l.6: '17?6' - 3rd digit: a 3/8-like body with the flag of a 5 written over it (correction); 1736 (a frequent group) or 1756/1786.
# NOTE l.7: '45?1' - after 4 and 5 (with its flag tick) comes a short bar hooked downward at its right end (top of a 7, or the bar of a 4) followed by a separate vertical stroke (1, or the descender of that 7): read 4571, 4541 or 457.
# NOTE l.8: 6440 - three short strokes under the 6 only (encoded '#' on the group); 2255 - last 5 retraced.
# NOTE l.10: '60?5' - 3rd digit retraced in dark ink, 8 written over a 7 (or vice versa): prob. 6085 (cf. 096R 6085), else 6075.
# NOTE l.12: 1009 - two strokes under 09, crossed by the tail of the 9 (=).
# NOTE l.14: 107 - three strokes under 07 (#); 2725 - underline under 25; 591 followed by a low dash (period).
