# Letter date: 25 Dec 1751 (from the heading; the place is not visible here - the r1953_1752 note cites Politische Correspondenz 8 no. 5253, Berlin 25 Dec 1751).
# NOTATION: _ single underline, = double, # triple; ! bar/overline; [del:...] struck; ? uncertain digit; {...} = editorial note on the preceding group (not part of it); "..." clear text.
# NOTE hand: NOT the copyist of 096R/097L/097R. Upright hand, groups separated by full stops (some commas); 7 = short top bar with a hooked/curled descender (looks like 'ʔ'); 1 = plain stroke, sometimes i-dotted (4170, 6211, 2104 - not encoded); 4 = open angular '4' with crossbar (not the 'a/u' form of 096R); 9 = closed loop with tail ('g'); 5 = 's' shape with flag; 0 small oval, 8 plain double loop. 0/8 and 1/7 were the main checks; every 7 vs 1 and 4 vs 7 decided at 4-5x.
# NOTE multiple underlines: 206 (l.3) and 2722 (l.6) have FIVE strokes, 4086 (l.4) FOUR strokes (counted at 6x on contrast-enhanced crops); all three encoded '#', with the stroke count in {}.
# NOTE l.1: the middle group is 144: both digits have the diagonal + crossbar + down-stroke of this hand's 4 (compare 448, 5814 in l.2), not the hooked 7 of 7./1736/6117.
# NOTE l.5: '1?20': the 2nd digit is a z-shape with a crossbar (top bar, diagonal, bottom bar, crossbar) - unlike every other 7 (hook form) and 2 (looped) on the page; probably 7 and 2 written over each other (1720 or 1220; 1220 is frequent: l.7, l.17, l.24).
# NOTE l.6: 2722 - the 3rd digit (2) is retraced in darker ink.

# NOTE l.21: 5797 - 3rd digit 9 blotted/retraced at the bowl (tail of 9 clear); 2?64 - 2nd digit 4 and 8 superimposed (dark 4 written over an 8, or vice versa): 2464 or 2864.
