J’ay fait icy le plu[s] de caresses que j’ay pu à Kerim Bey ; & quoique je n’aye pu avoir avec luy
aucune conférence, j’ay lieu d’estre content des disposition favorables qu’il a fait paroître pour
notre nation au personnes qui ont été luy parler de ma part. Il faut continuer de cultiver son amitié et
luy témoigner la satisfaction que j’ay des marques qu’il m’a donné de la sienne.
Translation
I have shown Kerim Bey as much favour as I could. Although I have been unable to confer with him, I
have reason to be pleased with the favourable disposition toward our nation that he showed to the
people who spoke to him on my behalf. You must continue to cultivate his friendship and tell him how
pleased I am with the signs he has given me of his own.
