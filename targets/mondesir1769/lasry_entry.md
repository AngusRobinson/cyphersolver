Translation
Even in his own profession, he is, one may say, ignorant, presumptuous, and stubborn as a Breton. [?] lets
himself be strongly prejudiced by the first person who comes along, clings to ideas hastily seized upon, and
puts them into action without reflection or consulting anyone. This produces many foolish decisions and
orders that have to be reversed or left without effect. [?] is impetuous, to the point of anger and excess.
