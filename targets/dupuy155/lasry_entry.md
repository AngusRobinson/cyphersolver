f41r, rows9-14
[ante]cessore et nel mio. Et se Vostra Altezza nel principio fusse contentata di por quel luogo in
mano di una terza persona confidente anbe le parti, perche lo conservassi a che fusse stato
legitimo et Catolico Re di Francia, come da Sua Maesta ella ne fu richiesta, che si saria rimediato
a molti inconvenienti et non sariano nati li a[ccidenti].
Translation
...my predecessor and in my own time. And if Your Highness had initially agreed to put that fortress in the
hands of a third person trusted by both sides, to preserve it for whoever became the legitimate Catholic King
of France, as His Majesty had requested, many difficulties could have been remedied and those incidents
would not have arisen.
