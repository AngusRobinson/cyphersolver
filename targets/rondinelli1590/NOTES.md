… dalla necessita io non posso credere che l signor duca di Mena sia bastante per difenderla,

Translation
…because of the necessity, I cannot believe that the Duke of Mena is capable of defending it, seeing that he
takes counsel and governs himself according to the advice of those who desire his ruin.
